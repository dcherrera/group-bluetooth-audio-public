Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Group Bluetooth Audio ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our macOS application.

Information We Collect

Local Device Information

Group Bluetooth Audio stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Device Preferences: Volume levels and mute states for each audio device
  • Device Configurations: Which devices you've grouped together for multi-output
  • App Settings: Your Pro subscription status, volume step preferences, and key mappings
  • Master Volume: The overall volume level you've set

The app accesses but does not store:

  • Audio Device Names: To display in the interface (not permanently stored)
  • Bluetooth Device Status: To show connection state (not permanently stored)

Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • Audio content or recordings
  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Browsing history or activity outside the app
  • Network traffic or internet activity
  • Files or documents on your computer

How We Use Information

The locally stored information is used exclusively for:

  • Remembering your preferred volume levels for each device
  • Restoring your device grouping configurations when you restart the app
  • Saving your Pro subscription status and custom settings
  • Maintaining your custom key mappings and preferences

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage Only

All data is stored locally on your Mac using:

  • SwiftData: For device volume levels, mute states, and grouping configurations
  • UserDefaults: For Pro subscription status, volume step preferences, and custom key mappings

No Remote Transmission

Group Bluetooth Audio does not transmit any data to external servers, third parties, or our systems. Your information never leaves your device.

Permissions

The app requests the following macOS permissions:

  • Bluetooth Access: To detect and manage Bluetooth audio devices
  • Audio Input: To create multi-output audio configurations
  • Apple Events: To control media playback (play/pause/next/previous)

These permissions are used solely for the app's core functionality and are not used to collect personal information.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Group Bluetooth Audio offers optional Pro features through in-app purchases, including an auto-renewable monthly subscription. Purchase information is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information.

For detailed information about our subscription terms, pricing, and duration, please review our Terms of Use.

Children's Privacy

Group Bluetooth Audio does not collect personal information from users of any age. The app is safe for users under 13 as it operates entirely locally without data collection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Third-Party Services

Group Bluetooth Audio does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app operates independently without external dependencies.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information:

  • Access: All your data is accessible through the app interface
  • Modification: You can change or delete preferences at any time
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
